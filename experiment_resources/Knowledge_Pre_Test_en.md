**1. Concept of Descriptive Statistics**:

***What is the main objective of descriptive statistics?***

A) Predicting the future behavior of a population based on a sample.

B) Describing and summarizing a collected data set without drawing conclusions about a broader population.

C) Determining cause and effect between two study variables.

D) Inferring the characteristics of all employees of a company from a small survey.

- **Correct answer:** B

- **Justification:** Literal definition from the video (0:27).

**2. Identifying the Mode:**

***If we survey 24 students at a school about how they get to school, and the following results are obtained: 5 people by bike, 12 by car, 5 on foot, and 2 by bus. What is the mode?***

A) Bike and on foot (Bimodal).

B) 12.

C) Car.

D) The average of the modes of transport.

- **Correct answer:** C

- **Justification:** Car has the highest frequency (12).

**3. Calculating the Median (Even N)**:

***We have the following set of grades from 4 students in a certain subject: 8, 2, 4, 10. What is the median?***

A) 4

B) 2

C) 6

D) 8.

- **Correct answer:** C

- **Justification:** Even: (4+8)/2 = 6.

**4. Definition of Standard Deviation:**

***Conceptually, what does the standard deviation tell us?***

A) The difference between the maximum and minimum value.

B) The value that appears most frequently.

C) The approximate average distance between each individual data point and the mean of the set.

D) The exact center of the data when it is sorted.

- **Correct answer:** C

- **Justification:** Definition of average distance to the mean.

**5. Contingency Tables**:

***What is a contingency table (crosstab) specifically used for?***

A) Showing the frequency of a single categorical variable.

B) Calculating the mean of a numeric variable.

C) Analyzing and comparing the relationship between two categorical variables simultaneously.

D) Visualizing the outliers of a distribution.

- **Correct answer:** C

- **Justification:** Definition of contingency table.

## Block II: Transfer (Application and Analysis)

**6. Robustness of Measures (Salary Scenario)**:

***Imagine a small startup with 5 employees. Four of them earn €1,000 a month, and the founder earns €20,000 a month. If you want to give a new candidate a realistic picture of the "typical" salary of a worker, which measure of central tendency should you use and why?***

A) The mean, because it includes the founder's salary and is mathematically more precise.

B) The median, because it is resistant to extreme values and better represents the majority group.

C) The mode, because there is no value that repeats.

D) The range, because it shows the full salary difference.

- **Correct answer:** B

- **Justification:** The median is robust to outliers (the founder).

**7. Interpreting the Interquartile Range (IQR):**

***A school report indicates that the Interquartile Range (IQR) of a class's grades goes from 50 to 80 points. What can we deduce?***

A) That 50% of the students scored between 50 and 80.

B) That the class's average grade is exactly 65.

C) That 25% of the students scored below 50.

D) That no one scored above 80 points.

- **Correct answer:** A

- **Justification:** IQR = Q3-Q1, contains the central 50%.

**8. Limitation of Description (Clinical Scenario):**

***A hospital conducts a descriptive study on the blood pressure of 20 patients admitted to a ward and calculates their mean. Based on the distinction between descriptive and inferential statistics, is it correct to state that "the average blood pressure of all the hospital's patients is that value"?***

A) Yes, because descriptive statistics allows generalization if the data is well measured.

B) No, because descriptive statistics only summarizes the collected data (the 20 patients) without drawing conclusions about the total population.

C) Yes, as long as we also calculate the standard deviation.

D) No, unless we use a contingency table.

- **Correct answer:** B

- **Justification:** Distinction between descriptive and inferential statistics.

**9. Calculating and Comparing Dispersion:**

***You have two groups of students in the same subject. Group A: Scores [8, 9, 10, 8, 9]. Group B: Scores [0, 10, 0, 10, 5]. Group A's average score is 8.8, Group B's is 5. Without doing complex calculations, which group will have a higher standard deviation?***

A) Group A, because its scores are higher and more consistent.

B) Group B, because its values are more spread out and further from its mean than those of Group A.

C) Group A, because its mean (8.8) is higher than Group B's (5).

D) With the data provided in the statement, the standard deviation formula cannot be fully applied, so the answer cannot be determined with complete accuracy.

- **Correct answer:** B

- **Justification:** Data far from the mean = higher SD.

**10. Chart Selection:**

***If the HR department wants to visualize what percentage of the total employees belongs to each department (Sales, IT, Marketing), which chart would be the most direct way to see "parts of a whole"?***

A) Histogram.

B) Box plot (Boxplot).

C) Pie chart.

D) Violin plot.

- **Correct answer:** C

- **Justification:** Pie chart for proportions/percentages.
