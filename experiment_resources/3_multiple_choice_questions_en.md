### Question 1
**Question:** Why is the median preferred over the mean in distributions with extremely high or low values?

**Options:**
1. Because calculating the median doesn't require sorting the data beforehand
2. Because the median always coincides with the mode in normal distributions
3. **Because the median is resistant to outliers, keeping its central value regardless of extremes ✅ (CORRECT)**
4. Because the arithmetic mean is unable to process decimal values

**Correct answer:** Because the median is resistant to outliers, keeping its central value regardless of extremes

**Explanation:** The video visually demonstrates that if the height of a person at the extreme increases drastically, the median remains unchanged in the central position, while the mean shifts significantly upward (02:04-02:28).

**Interval:** 00:02:03 - 00:02:28

### Question 2
**Question:** What is the fundamental distinction the video makes between descriptive and inferential statistics?

**Options:**
1. **Descriptive statistics only summarizes the data collected, while inferential statistics draws conclusions about a broader population ✅ (CORRECT)**
2. Descriptive statistics is for small samples and inferential statistics is for full population censuses
3. Descriptive statistics only uses tables and inferential statistics only uses advanced charts
4. Inferential statistics focuses on summarizing past data and descriptive statistics on predicting the future

**Correct answer:** Descriptive statistics only summarizes the data collected, while inferential statistics draws conclusions about a broader population

**Explanation:** It is explained that knowing how some employees commute (descriptive) doesn't allow us to state how all the company's workers commute without resorting to inferential statistics (00:34-00:59).

**Interval:** 00:00:34 - 00:00:59

### Question 3
**Question:** What percentage of the data is contained within the interquartile range?

**Options:**
1. 100% of the data excluding the minimum values
2. The lowest 25% of the values
3. **The central 50% of the data, located between the first and third quartile ✅ (CORRECT)**
4. 75% of the total distribution

**Correct answer:** The central 50% of the data, located between the first and third quartile

**Explanation:** The interquartile range is defined as the difference between Q1 and Q3, leaving out the lower 25% and upper 25% of the sample, thereby capturing the central half of the observations (04:47-05:12).

**Interval:** 00:04:47 - 00:05:12
