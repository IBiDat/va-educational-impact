**1. Scope of Descriptive Statistics**

***According to the video, if we analyze the race times of 10 athletes from a club, descriptive statistics allows us to:***

A) State how long athletes from other clubs will take to run.

B) Demonstrate why some run faster than others.

C) Summarize and describe the times of those 10 specific athletes.

D) Calculate the probability of winning the next race.

- **Correct answer:** C

- **Justification:** Definition applied to a context (athletes).

**2. Identifying the Mode**

***In a survey about favorite colors, the following results were obtained: 3 people chose Blue, 3 chose Green, 10 chose Red, and 4 chose Yellow. What is the mode of this set?***

A) Blue and Green.

B) 10.

C) Red.

D) 5 (the average of the choices).

- **Correct answer:** C

- **Justification:** Red has the highest frequency (10).

**3. Calculating the Median (Even N)**:

***We have two 4-story buildings, whose floors have the following m²: Building A: 20 m², 10 m², 40 m², 30 m². Building B: 7.5 m², 30 m², 20 m², 90 m². What are the medians of the 2 buildings?***

A) Building A: 20 m². Building B: 30 m².

B) 30 m² both.

C) 25 m² both.

D) Building A: 15 m². Building B: 30 m².

- **Correct answer:** C

- **Justification:** Even: (20+30)/2 = 25.

**4. Interpreting Standard Deviation:**

***According to what is explained in the video, what fundamental characteristic of the data does the standard deviation describe?***

A) The accuracy or absence of errors in data collection.

B) The average degree of dispersion or spread of the values around the mean.

C) The overall average value of all the data added together.

D) The total amount of data that differs from the most frequent value.

- **Correct answer:** B

- **Justification:** Definition of average distance to the mean.

**5. Frequency Tables**

***What is the main function of a simple frequency table as described in the video?***

A) Showing how often each distinct value appears in a single variable.

**6. Robustness of Measures (Real Estate Scenario)**

***In a neighborhood, almost all houses cost around €100,000, but there is one mansion that costs €5,000,000. If you want to know the price of a "standard" house in that neighborhood in order to buy one, why wouldn't the mean be a good reference?***

A) Because the mean will ignore the price of the mansion.

B) Because the mean is a measure of dispersion, not of central tendency.

C) Because the mean will be much higher than the actual price of most houses due to the outlier.

D) Because the mean is always lower than the median in these cases.

- **Correct answer:** C

- **Justification:** The mean is sensitive to outliers (the mansion).

**7. Interpreting the Interquartile Range (IQR)**:

***In a salary analysis, the first quartile (Q1) is €1,500 and the third quartile (Q3) is €2,500. What information does the Interquartile Range give us about the employees?***

A) That employees earn between €1,500 and €2,500 on average.

B) That 50% of employees have salaries between €1,500 and €2,500.

C) That there is a €1,000 difference between the highest and lowest earner.

D) That the most frequent salary is €2,000.

- **Correct answer:** B

- **Justification:** IQR delimits the central 50% of the data.

**8. Limitation of Description (School Scenario):**

***A teacher calculates the average grade of the 30 students in his math class (Class A). Can he use that single descriptive figure to state that "the students in Class B have the same performance"?***

A) Yes, if the classes are comparable.

B) No, descriptive statistics only describes the group analyzed (Class A); extending it to Class B would be inference.

C) Yes, the mean is a universal estimator.

D) No, because he hasn't calculated the mode of Class A.

- **Correct answer:** B

- **Justification:** Distinction between descriptive and inferential statistics.

**9. Calculating and Comparing Dispersion:**

***Imagine two bus routes to work. Route 1: Always takes 30 minutes (30, 30, 30, 30). Route 2: Sometimes takes 10 minutes and sometimes 50 minutes (10, 50, 10, 50). Both have an average of 30 minutes. Which route has a higher standard deviation, and what does that imply for your punctuality?***

A) Route 1, because consistency increases the deviation.

B) Both have the same deviation since they have the same mean.

C) Route 2 has a higher deviation; it is less predictable and the data is further from the mean.

D) With the data provided in the statement, the standard deviation formula cannot be fully applied, so the answer cannot be determined with complete accuracy.

- **Correct answer:** C

- **Justification:** Variable data (10, 50) = higher SD.
