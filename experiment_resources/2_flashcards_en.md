### Flashcard 1

### Flashcard 2
**Concept:** Arithmetic Mean
**Description:** Represents the average of the set and is calculated by summing all observations and dividing by the total number of observations (n) (01:22-01:28).
**Interval:** 00:01:21 - 00:01:28

---

### Flashcard 3
**Concept:** Median
**Description:** The middle value when the data is sorted in ascending order. Its most notable property is its resistance, or robustness, to outliers or extreme values (01:46-02:08).
**Interval:** 00:01:44 - 00:02:08

---

### Flashcard 4
**Concept:** Mode
**Description:** Refers to the value or values that appear most frequently in a data distribution, being especially useful for qualitative variables (02:30-02:35).
**Interval:** 00:02:29 - 00:02:35

---

### Flashcard 5
**Concept:** Standard Deviation
**Description:** A measure of dispersion that indicates the average distance between each individual data point and the mean of the set, reflecting the degree of spread relative to the center (03:06-03:11).
**Interval:** 00:03:04 - 00:03:11
